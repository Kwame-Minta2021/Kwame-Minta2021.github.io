## About Farmlynco

Farmlynco is an AI-powered platform for rice farmers that provides market access, weather forecasting and local-language support.

## Yield prediction

The models predict crop yield by combining noisy, sparse on-farm sensor data with weather APIs and satellite features.

## Irrigation decision support

The irrigation decision-support system achieved 30% water savings in deployment.
